ComposeCFM harmonizes multi-site brain MRI markers by composing conditional flow maps for site, scanner and software effects. Joint work with Tianxi Li, Mingzhao Hu (Mayo Clinic) and Xiaotong Shen. This work was selected as one of 15 finalists of ARISE 2026–2027. About the ARISE series: [Amstat News](https://magazine.amstat.org/blog/2026/06/01/sdsa/).
